Short clips (owl 9.7s, frog 13.1s, cicada 25.1s, cat purring 35.9s) are **not** ten-minute independently recorded masters. They remain research candidates and should not be silently looped and relabeled as long original field recordings.

## Release gates
- Download only from permitted source account/endpoint and preserve public page metadata, creator, license and hash.
- Measure **independent unique duration**; verify whether a page file is already looped.
- Inspect stereo/mono and bandwidth, measure LUFS and true peak, detect clicks/events on 10-minute master.
- Audition through review UI with scrubbing near end and phone/background playback.
- Explicit owner listening approval and release review before linking any file to production.
- Keep 35 current catalog source IDs, 45 presets, live URLs, HLS, R2 and existing Sound Pages untouched.
