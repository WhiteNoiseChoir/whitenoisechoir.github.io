# WhiteNoiseChoir audio library — public beta

There are **20 user-selectable sound sources** in the player:

- 15 original synthesized noise / ambient-demo MP3 files;
- 5 distinct real-world field recordings, sourced from Freesound and marked **CC0 1.0** by the respective uploaders.

Recording filenames end in `-real.mp3`; synthetic prototypes keep their original filenames.

Each original CC0 page, creator, source preview, content checksum and processed filename is documented in [cc0-sources.json](cc0-sources.json) and on the [public license page](https://whitenoisechoir.github.io/sources.html).

The CC0 sounds were processed into approximately 54-second stereo loops, level-adjusted and stored directly on GitHub Pages. No recordings were copied from YouTube.

**Quality control:** The original issue was that many nature tracks were different filters of broadband noise. The five prominent nature/machine tracks were replaced with independent field recordings. The remaining simulations are intentionally labeled as synthetic and need further listening review. Human listening QA, background playback and seamless-loop QA on actual devices are not complete.
