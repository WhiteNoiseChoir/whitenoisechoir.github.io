White Noise, Your Way

White noise has equal power per unit bandwidth. Pink and brown noise emphasize lower frequencies in different ways. Choose the sound that you find comfortable. Our sounds are not medical treatments. Natural recordings are marked CC0; other ambient experiments are marked Synthetic. Start at low volume and avoid placing a speaker close to a sleeping infant.

For compatible browsers, our long sound masters use a native continuous audio stream; other browsers use MP3 looping. The sound should continue until Pause/Stop or the selected sleep timer. Background playback depends on iOS and the browser, and system interruptions can still stop audio. The timer uses the device clock; iOS may delay stopping audio while suspended, so it is not a safety cutoff. Dark screen mode only blacks out this webpage.
