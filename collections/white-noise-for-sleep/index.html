How to choose

White Noise has a brighter hiss, Pink Noise rolls off the highs, Brown Noise emphasizes lows, Soft White Noise provides an intentionally softened texture, while our Green Noise uses a separate mid-band filter shape (currently awaiting real-device listening review).

Listening tips

Use the sleep timer only as a convenience, not a safety cutoff on an unattended iOS device.

A sleep timer is a convenience, not a device-safe cutoff when a phone is suspended. Sessions can also be interrupted by the operating system or browser.
