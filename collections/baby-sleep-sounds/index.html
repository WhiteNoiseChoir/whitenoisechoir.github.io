Baby Sleep Sounds

A quieter, simple sound source may be preferable to complex ambiences with sharp changes, but there is no universal safe file-volume setting.

Baby listening safety: Place the sound source away from the crib, use low volume, and consider the listening duration. Audio loudness in LUFS is not a substitute for a measured sound level at the baby's ears.

How to choose

Select a gentle track, position the playback device away from the sleeping area, and use the lowest practical volume.

Listening tips

Digital LUFS or percentage volume cannot tell you the sound pressure level measured near a baby's ears.

A sleep timer is a convenience, not a device-safe cutoff when a phone is suspended. Sessions can also be interrupted by the operating system or browser.
