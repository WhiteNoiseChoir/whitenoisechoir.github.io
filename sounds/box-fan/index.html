An authentic mechanical background

What is different about a real Box Fan recording?

This sound comes from a recorded box fan rather than from a digitally simulated fan algorithm. The source is credited to ffdm on Freesound under CC0. It can contain the kind of airflow and motor-texture variation that a synthesized approximation might simplify, though the exact perceived detail depends on your playback device.

Unlike our Desk Fan — Synthetic file, which is an original ten-minute DSP render, this master contains 67.52 seconds of independent real-world recording. Continuous listening repeats that cycle. You may hear its recurring pattern more readily than the much longer synthetic track.

Fan recording versus air-conditioner noise

Air Conditioner — Synthetic is another digitally created background, not a recorded air-conditioner make or model. A real fan and a synthetic air-conditioner can have overlapping steady-spectrum regions while still sounding different on actual speakers. Select by ear, without assuming the names imply the same recording or a specific appliance specification.

Questions about Real Box Fan Sound

Is this recording made from a physical box fan?

Yes. This is an edited CC0 field recording credited to ffdm, not an original fan-synthesis preset generated by WhiteNoiseChoir.

Does the fan recording last for ten minutes before repeating?

No. The independent audio in this master is 67.52 seconds long. The browser repeats that material for longer listening via native HLS or MP3 looping.

How is it different from the Desk Fan — Synthetic track?

Desk Fan — Synthetic is independently generated DSP with a ten-minute source. Box Fan is an actual fan recording with a much shorter source cycle. The two sources are separate and are labeled honestly.
