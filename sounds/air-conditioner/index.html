What does Air Conditioner — Synthetic sound like?

This independent DSP master emphasizes lower-frequency background energy and a relatively stable envelope, with its measured overall spectral balance documented below. It is not a simulation calibrated to a brand or appliance model.

How it differs from related sounds

Compared with Desk Fan, this version aims for an indoor cooling-room impression rather than a small fan's blade and motor character. Low Rumble is darker again and less explicitly machine-like.

Choosing a listening situation

Use it as an unobtrusive room-like backdrop, but compare the sound itself instead of assuming it matches an actual AC compressor cycle. The file does not cool a room or replace household equipment.

Questions about Air Conditioner — Synthetic

Is this an actual AC recording?

No. This recording is independently synthesized. We do not claim to have captured a physical air conditioner.

Does it include realistic compressor on/off cycles?

It is a continuous background texture, not a verified model-specific sequence of compressor starts or stops.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
