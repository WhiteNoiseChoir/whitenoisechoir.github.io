Questions about Neutral Mid Noise

Is Neutral Mid Noise a recognized acoustic standard?

No. It is a WhiteNoiseChoir descriptive name for this original DSP master.

Does it have the same band-pass filters as Green Noise?

No. Green Noise is a separate master with declared filter cutoffs. We do not attribute those settings to this file.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
