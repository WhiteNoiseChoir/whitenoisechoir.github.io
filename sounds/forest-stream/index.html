What makes this recording distinctive?

Recorded along the Jadwiżanka stream at Lądek-Zdrój, Poland, in September 2021. This is a separate, authentic stereo field source rather than a pitch-shifted or filtered version of Rushing River.

Forest Stream offers another natural flow texture distinct from the original Waterfall and powerful Rushing River. Whether it feels more gentle is subjective; the title is not a measurable sound level guarantee.

Original field recording and CC0 license

Recorded by GirlWithSoundRecorder and released under Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0). Read the original Freesound recording and licensing page. The approved WhiteNoiseChoir audio was made from the creator's publicly available lossy Freesound HQ MP3 preview, not the original WAV. Encoding at 192 kbps does not restore fidelity already lost.

Original source duration: approximately 901.0 seconds. Independently selected field source used: 605 seconds. Approved master: 600 seconds, 44.1 kHz, stereo, 192 kbps MP3. The same exact SHA-256 approved listening-review master was used in production without re-encoding.

How continuous playback works

The ten-minute master uses approximately 605 seconds of distinct recorded sound with a short circular seam; no separate short section is repeated within the first ten-minute arrangement. A seven-day static HLS VOD playlist repeats the whole 600-second audio cycle for long listening on native-HLS devices. Other browsers use MP3 loop fallback. The static playlist is not an infinite livestream, and iOS lock-screen behavior remains device-dependent.

Natural fluctuations may include louder nearby splashes or environmental sounds. Start softly rather than compensating for quiet passages. This is a listening aid, not a medical treatment. Keep volume comfortable, particularly around children and infants.
