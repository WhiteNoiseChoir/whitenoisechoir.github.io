Why does Brown Noise sound deeper?

Its power spectral density is approximately proportional to 1/f²: for every tenfold increase in frequency, the average power level falls about 20 dB. A phone speaker may not reproduce the deepest bass, so the same audio can sound noticeably different through headphones and speakers.

Brown Noise questions

What is Brown Noise?

Brown noise, also called red noise in some contexts, emphasizes lower frequencies: its power generally declines proportional to frequency squared. It is not simply white noise played at a lower volume.

How is it different from Pink Noise?

Pink noise falls approximately 10 dB per frequency decade, whereas Brown Noise falls approximately 20 dB per decade. Pink generally sounds brighter than Brown. Use the comparison above to decide what you prefer.

Does Brown Noise improve sleep?

Some people use it as a steady background when winding down, but research does not establish a guaranteed sleep benefit or treatment for insomnia. Keep playback comfortably quiet.

Will it stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes is the master file duration, not the listening session limit. The player loops or uses a pre-authored HLS timeline; device/network interruptions may still occur.

Can it play when my iPhone screen is locked?

Supported Safari devices use native HLS playback designed for longer background sessions. Behavior still depends on iOS and should be checked on your actual device.
