How to choose a sound

Choose a noise color for a uniform synthesized background, or explore machine-like and environmental textures for a more specific listening character. Sound labels distinguish independent original DSP from field recordings and disclose when a ten-minute arrangement was built from a shorter source.

All available sources have their own page and continuous listening controls. A file that passes automated checks may still require subjective approval for long sessions; planned sounds are never presented as playable masters.
