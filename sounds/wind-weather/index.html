Wind-like DSP versus true weather recordings

Gentle Wind is original synthetic airflow, not microphone capture of wind through trees, coastal gusts or snow. Natural wind often contains gust variation, microphone turbulence and environmental details absent from a steady synthetic texture.

The dedicated page reports the actual measured spectral tilt so its darker frequency character can be evaluated honestly. Outdoor wind recordings will only be added as their own audio sources when verified.
