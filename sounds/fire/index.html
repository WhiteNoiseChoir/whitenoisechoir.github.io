An authentic crackle, edited for continuous listening

Fireplace is sourced from a real CC0 recording, with intermittent pops and changes in intensity. The ten-minute published master was arranged from about one minute of independent recorded material, not ten new minutes of a burning fire.

People who like gradual changes and transients may prefer recorded crackling over uniform noise. The longer arrangement still requires listening review, and new Campfire or Wood Stove sources are not published until distinct recordings exist.
