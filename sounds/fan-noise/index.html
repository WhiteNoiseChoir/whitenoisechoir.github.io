About this category

Steady fans combine airflow noise with motor tones. Different shapes and speeds can change the perceived pitch and rhythm, which is why recordings should not be interchangeable under different names.

Recorded fan versus simulated airflow

A field recording captures the sound of a physical motor, airflow, room and microphone together. Our Desk Fan master is a separately generated DSP approximation; the Box Fan source is an authentic but short CC0 recording. We do not label the synthetic track as a real fan.

For an extended, steady synthetic texture choose Desk Fan. If realism and incidental mechanical details matter more, preview Box Fan while remembering that its independent recorded material is short. Longer authentic recordings remain in the production queue.
