What makes Rain on Tent different?

Inside a mountain tent, water strikes flexible material rather than a hard metal or glass surface. You may hear a softer, more diffuse pattern of small impacts, interrupted by occasional individual drops. The original field recording was made with a Zoom H2 and is not a synthesized rain texture.

Rain on Tin Roof has sharper metallic impacts. Rain on Window emphasizes glass patter, while Light Rain is an outdoor rain bed.

Original source and actual recording quality

Recorded by venaportae and shared as Creative Commons Zero 1.0 (CC0). View original Freesound recording and license details. WhiteNoiseChoir used the publicly accessible compressed HQ MP3 preview, not the original lossless WAV. A 192 kbps MP3 encode cannot restore fidelity already lost from the preview.

Original recording length: approximately 131 seconds. Independently selected source used in this arrangement: approximately 125 seconds. Approved master: 600 seconds, 44.1 kHz, 2 channels, 192 kbps MP3. The original file and the ten-minute listening master are distinct products and should not be confused.

Repetition and long listening sessions

Approximately 125 seconds of distinct field audio were arranged into the 600-second master using short crossfades. This repeats recognizable events about five times; the listening session can be longer than the length of the independently recorded sound.

The Safari/iPhone audio route uses a seven-day static HLS VOD playlist that repeats this approved ten-minute source segment. Other browsers use looping MP3. This is not an infinite stream, and locked-screen behavior still depends on iOS and the listening device. The master is never falsely presented as hours of new unique recording.

Comfortable playback

Start at a low volume, especially with headphones and while falling asleep. Natural rain and thunder contain changing transient levels. If sudden impacts interrupt rest, choose a steadier option such as Light Rain, Pink Noise or White Noise. This listening tool does not provide a medical treatment or guaranteed sleep effect. Keep sound sources a safe distance from infants and avoid prolonged loud sound exposure.
