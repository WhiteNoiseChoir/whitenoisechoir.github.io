About this category

Rivers and streams offer moving water with different current strengths, surf rises and falls along a shoreline, waterfall water is relatively dense, and synthetic Shower models a different, smoother water texture. All real recordings come from distinct CC0 original sources.

Choosing your next sound

Compare Rushing River with Forest Stream and Bodetal Brook & Birds for distinct current and outdoor textures, or Gentle Ocean Waves with Crashing Ocean Waves and original Ocean Waves for surf intensity. Waterfall and synthetic Shower offer different falling-water patterns. Gentle Ocean Waves includes occasional gulls and is not a gull-free ocean bed.

Compare water textures by source type

Shower is a digitally generated water-like sound. Ocean Waves and Waterfall use shorter, owner-approved CC0 field recordings; Rushing River, Forest Stream, Bodetal Brook & Birds, Gentle Ocean Waves and Crashing Ocean Waves add longer independent source segments. These are eight distinct acoustic concepts and should not be treated as interchangeable labels.

Waves rise and fall, waterfalls can have a dense broad wash, and a synthetic Shower approximates a more continuous splash texture. Source authenticity and independent recording length are disclosed on every sound page.
