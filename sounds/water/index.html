Shower is a digitally generated water-like sound. Ocean Waves and Waterfall use CC0 field recordings currently available in short production cuts. These are three distinct acoustic concepts and should not be treated as interchangeable labels.

Waves rise and fall, waterfalls can have a dense broad wash, and a synthetic Shower approximates a more continuous splash texture. Source authenticity and independent recording length are disclosed on every sound page.
