Bass weighting and playback hardware

Low Rumble is a steeply bass-weighted digital background. The measured spectral tilt is much darker than White Noise; small mobile speakers often fail to reproduce its lowest frequencies.

The audio is not a calibrated bass test or an aircraft recording. Compare it with Deep Brown Noise and Airplane Cabin at comfortable volume before deciding which low-frequency background you prefer.
