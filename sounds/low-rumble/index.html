What does Low Rumble sound like?

The measured spectrum has a steep negative tilt, so much of its energy sits relatively low in frequency. This can make the recording sound subdued on phone speakers with limited bass reproduction.

How it differs from related sounds

Questions about Low Rumble

Is Low Rumble the same as a subwoofer test?

No. It is a listening texture, not a calibrated single-frequency test tone or speaker-performance measurement.

Why do I hear less rumble through an iPhone speaker?

Small speakers often attenuate low frequencies. That is a device limitation rather than proof the file contains no energy.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
