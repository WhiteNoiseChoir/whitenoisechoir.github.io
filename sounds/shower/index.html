What does Shower — Synthetic sound like?

The measured master places considerable power at higher frequencies, which can make the texture sound sharper than a waterfall or Brown Noise recording. This is a synthetic profile rather than the acoustics of a measured shower head.

How it differs from related sounds

Rain on Window is based on real drops striking glass; Waterfall and Ocean Waves come from short field recordings; Shower is an independently synthesized sound. They are distinct sources and should not be treated as interchangeable water labels.

Choosing a listening situation

Try this when you want a consistent, water-like background without the stop-and-start structure of a natural scene. Begin at low volume; bright water-like sounds can feel prominent on headphones.

Questions about Shower — Synthetic

Was this recorded in a bathroom?

No. Shower is an original synthesized approximation of water noise, not a recorded fixture.

Why does it sound brighter than some other water sounds?

Its measured frequency distribution places more energy toward upper frequencies. Device response and volume can change how sharp it feels.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
