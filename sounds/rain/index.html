About this category

What makes rain-on-window distinctive?

Recorded rain contains changing droplets and impacts that are absent from stationary filtered noise. Rain on Window is assembled from about 60 seconds of authentic CC0 field material; Heavy Rain is a shorter separate recording still awaiting a longer approved source.

Compared with a synthetic Shower or Pink Noise, real rain can contain irregular transients. Choose based on how even or varied you want the background to feel, not because any one source guarantees sleep improvement.
