About this category

Rain sounds differ by how drops land, the size and rhythm of impacts, and the presence of wind or thunder. The source recordings span very different independent durations, from about 145 seconds of thunder to a 605-second rain segment. All source limitations and processing are disclosed.

Choosing your next sound

Try Light Rain for a gentler bed, Tin Roof for distinct metallic impacts, Distant Thunder for occasional low-frequency rumbles, Rain on Window for glass patter, or Heavy Rain for a denser texture. All five use separate CC0 field recordings.

What makes rain-on-window distinctive?

Recorded rain contains changing droplets and impacts that are absent from stationary filtered noise. Rain on Window is assembled from about 60 seconds of authentic CC0 field material; Heavy Rain uses a separately approved source of about 3 minutes 10 seconds. Three new sources add less-dense rain, metallic roof impacts, and spaced thunder events.

Compared with a synthetic Shower or Pink Noise, real rain can contain irregular transients. Choose based on how even or varied you want the background to feel, not because any one source guarantees sleep improvement.
