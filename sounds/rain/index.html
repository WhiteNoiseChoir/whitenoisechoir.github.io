About this category

Rain sounds differ by how drops land, the size and rhythm of impacts, and the presence of wind or thunder. The newly released sources span as little as approximately 125 seconds of original tent rain through 605 seconds of distinct rolling storm material. Recorded patterns may repeat during long listening. All source limitations and processing are disclosed.

Choosing your next sound

Try Light Rain for a gentler bed, Tent for a textile impact, Car Roof for enclosed vehicle resonance, Tin Roof for metallic impacts, Distant Thunder for spaced rumble and Rolling Thunder for a more dynamic outdoor storm. Rain on Window and Heavy Rain offer separate textures. All ten sound IDs are sourced from different CC0 recordings. Rain on Leaves uses 605 seconds of independent stereo source; Rain on Lake repeats around 343 seconds of original water-surface rainfall.

What makes rain-on-window distinctive?

Recorded rain contains changing droplets and impacts that are absent from stationary filtered noise. Rain on Window is assembled from about 60 seconds of authentic CC0 field material; Heavy Rain uses a separately approved source of about 3 minutes 10 seconds. Eight added sources now cover light rain, metallic roof impact, distant thunder, fabric tent patter, car roof resonance, rolling storms, rain on foliage and rain hitting lake water.

Compared with a synthetic Shower or Pink Noise, real rain can contain irregular transients. Choose based on how even or varied you want the background to feel, not because any one source guarantees sleep improvement.
