Machine-like sound without a real appliance

Air Conditioner is an original ten-minute DSP texture that evokes indoor ventilation and cooling hum. It does not come from a physical air-conditioning unit. Desk Fan is another independently generated source, with its own spectral character.

Field-recorded household appliances often contain changing compressor states, motor harmonics and room acoustics. Those realistic details should be verified before a recording is sold as an authentic machine sound.
