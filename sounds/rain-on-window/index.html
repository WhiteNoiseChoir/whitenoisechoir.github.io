What does Rain on Window sound like?

Unlike synthetic noise colors, rainfall contains changing impacts and small transients. The master may feel less uniform than White or Pink Noise because the recorded events are naturally irregular.

How it differs from related sounds

The underlying recorded material is authentic, but the resulting master is not ten independent minutes of captured weather. We disclose this distinction rather than implying a continuous ten-minute field session.

Choosing a listening situation

Try the recording for a textured natural background at moderate volume. If the raindrop transients distract you, a steadier synthesized noise master may be more comfortable.

Questions about Rain on Window

Is the rain on this page a real recording?

Yes. The source is a CC0 field recording, not a synthetic rain simulation.

Does the ten-minute MP3 contain ten minutes of unique weather recording?

No. About 60 seconds of independent recorded material were rearranged and crossfaded into the longer master.

Can it play for more than ten minutes?

Yes. The full ten-minute arrangement repeats through HLS or MP3 looping until playback is stopped or interrupted.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
