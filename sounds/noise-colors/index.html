About this category

White noise distributes power broadly across frequencies; pink noise emphasizes lower bands relative to white, while brown noise places even more energy toward bass frequencies. Blue and violet weight the treble instead.

Choosing your next sound

Which noise color feels most comfortable depends on the listener and their playback equipment. Use similar volume levels when comparing options.
