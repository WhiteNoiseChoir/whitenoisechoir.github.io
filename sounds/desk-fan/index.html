Questions about Desk Fan — Synthetic

Was this sound recorded from a real desk fan?

No. It is an original synthesized fan-like approximation; the source label is deliberately transparent.

Why can a synthetic fan sound feel more even?

The DSP is not constrained by room reflections and incidental environmental sounds in a real field recording. Realism still requires subjective listening approval.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
