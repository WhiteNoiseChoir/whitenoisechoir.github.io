What does Fireplace sound like?

Unlike stationary noise colors, the sound includes individual short transients with variable timing. The full master is assembled from roughly 60 seconds of independent field audio rather than ten fresh minutes of continuous fire recording.

How it differs from related sounds

The occasional pops and space between crackles may feel more dynamic than steady rain or Brown Noise. This is part of the original recording, not evidence of a faulty loop.

Choosing a listening situation

Use it for a quiet cozy background, or compare with Rain on Window if you prefer many small water impacts. Users sensitive to transient pops may prefer a steadier source.

Questions about Fireplace

Is this a real fireplace recording?

Yes. It is based on an authentic CC0 field recording, not a DSP fire effect.

Is the recording ten minutes of independent fire sounds?

No. The original independent material is about one minute; the production edit is ten minutes long.

Is the audio actually heating the room?

No. This is a digital sound recording only, not a connected heater or a functional fire simulator.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
