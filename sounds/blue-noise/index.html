What does Blue Noise sound like?

An ideal blue-noise power spectrum increases roughly in proportion to frequency. Its measured spectral tilt in this master is close to +10 dB per frequency decade, which is substantially brighter than the near-flat White Noise master.

How it differs from related sounds

The extra treble can be useful for exploring the way frequency balance changes the perceived sound, but it may be fatiguing at higher levels. Pink and Brown Noise are better places to start if you prefer a softer, darker background.

Questions about Blue Noise

Is Blue Noise brighter than White Noise?

Yes, in the idealized spectral sense: Blue Noise puts proportionally more energy at higher frequencies. Perceived loudness still depends on the device and volume.

Should I leave Blue Noise playing all night?

There is no general reason to assume bright noise is suitable for everyone. Keep the volume low, listen for discomfort, and choose a gentler texture if preferred.

Does the sound stop after ten minutes?

No. Ten minutes describes the MP3 master; the player uses continuous HLS or MP3 looping until stopped, the timer expires, or your device interrupts playback. HLS repeats the master rather than creating additional independent audio.
